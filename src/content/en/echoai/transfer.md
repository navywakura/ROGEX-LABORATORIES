# TRANSFER-3 — contract and state

Updated: 18 September 2026. **Red, no candidate.**

Demonstrate that learning in A improves behaviour in other environments against the same agent without that experience, without carrying a map or solution. Budget, sensors, actuators and constraints are matched between arms. The exam freezes learning while perception and tracking remain active.

B3: 24 public shapes, three conditions per shape; 51/72 arrivals versus nominal's 52/72. School+exam energy 7100 versus 6936, simulated units. Zero observed safety violations in every arm; five nominal arrivals are lost. All three B2/B3 audits reproduce their hashes; 261 tests pass. The global suite remains unverified because of memory limits.

## Gates for green

1. Public screening must show usefulness, respect constraints and beat appropriate controls including programmed priors.
2. A candidate must pass prospective confirmation with sample size, criteria and rules frozen before viewing fresh rooms.
3. The final campaign requires commitments published before training, external custody and a single opening of B, followed by C only if B passes. Failure remains recorded.

No fresh B2/B3 confirmation ran; no real B/C was generated or opened. Custody is prepared once a candidate exists. There is no hardware: this demonstrates neither physical transfer nor an instrumented battery.
