# ECHO-4.5 · technical documentation (in development)

Updated 26 September 2026. ECHO-4.5 is **not released yet**: this page documents how it is being
built and what has been measured so far. Every phase has a contract with its green criterion written
**before** measuring, and it is published even when it comes out red.

## What it is

ECHO-4.5 starts from an **untrained ECHO seed** that is cloned into sandboxes and learns in them. The
goal is for it to acquire **verified concepts** and reuse them on new problems. It is measured on
ARC-AGI-1, ARC-AGI-2 and ARC-AGI-3.

| Piece | Role |
|---|---|
| **Seed** | a vocabulary of small operations on grids: geometry, objects, gravity, halves, sections, colours. Today it has 84 families |
| **Sandbox** | a copy with its own concept library, learning curve and **hash-chained journal** (every verified program and every concept born) |
| **Awake** | program search; only what reproduces every solved example is a **fact** |
| **Sleep** | what repeats across programs of different tasks becomes a new **concept**, also with colour slots ("recolour X to Y") |
| **Intuition** | integer costs that make it try first what has worked most often; optionally, a network trained on a GPU on "dreamt" tasks |
| **Gym** | 1009 training tasks from ARC-AGI-1 and ARC-AGI-2. 201 are **held out** to measure transfer. The evaluations are **sealed by code** |
| **Game agent** | the `echo-arc` package for ARC-AGI-3: verified memory, BOUNDARY, the game clock, clicks inside the object and discovery of its own body |

## Phases and results

| Phase | Green criterion (fixed beforehand) | Result |
|---|---|---|
| S0 · seed, sandboxes and gym | working and audited | ✅ |
| S1 · objects in grids | more held-out tasks than the seed | ✅ 15 → **21** |
| S2 · learning better | more than 21 with deterministic search | ✅ **24** |
| S3 · games as objects | higher official score than v2, same levels or more, exact replay | ✅ 0.506 → **0.678** (7 levels) |
| S4 · inferring the goal and planning | more levels and a higher score than S3 | ✅ in development (iteration 3, v5: 0.849 mean over 3 seeds) |
| S5 · game simulator (ARC3-GYM) | v5 beats v3 on new games | 🔴 original contract · ✅ S5-D exam: v5.2 1103 levels against 940 |
| S6 · self-improvement: ECHO asks a model for operations and verifies them | ≥ 27 held-out, and more than the fixed request | ⏳ running |

**Details that matter:**
- **Deterministic search:** the per-task time limit was removed, because it made the result depend
  on CPU load. Now two measurements give exactly the same set of tasks.
- **Budget was not the ceiling:** 1500 expansions solve the same tasks as 400. The limit was the
  vocabulary.
- **Intuition network:** trained on a Kaggle T4 GPU with 150,000 tasks generated by ECHO from real
  training grids. It is right 79 % of the time among its top 5 suggestions on invented tasks, but on
  the held-out tasks it ties with the frequency intuition.
- **S4:** in the game g50t, the goal needs a causal mechanic ("a copy on the switch opens the gate").
  The third iteration adds detecting causes, checking them by acting and planning in two steps. Since
  the second iteration it is measured with **3 seeds per game**, because with a single one luck
  weighed more than improvement.

## EGO-MEMORY-1 (in parallel)

A simulated village of agents, some of them liars, where ECHO's memory is wiped between episodes and
writing in the journal costs time. It measures whether the habit of documenting **emerges out of
need**, and whether memories with an integer valence (+ or −) **cause** its behaviour. In
development, with 8 seeds:
- the journal saves it time in 8 of 8;
- deleting the formative memory changes whom it picks in 8 of 8, and deleting a random one does not;
- two identical clones end up with different stances in 8 of 8.

It is a **functional** self; it does not demonstrate consciousness.

## Final ECHO-4.5 exam

| | Requirement |
|---|---|
| R1 · ARC-AGI-1 (400 evaluation tasks) | above 0 % and above the untrained seed |
| R2 · ARC-AGI-2 (120 evaluation tasks) | above 0 % and above the seed |
| R3 · ARC-AGI-3 (16 games not used for development) | complete at least 20 % of the 112 levels |
| R4 | full audit: programs, concepts and journal chain |

The official ARC-AGI-3 score is always published: each level is squared and levels are weighted by
their number.
