# ECHO-AI — ECHO-1 versus ECHO-2 results

ECHO-1 and ECHO-2 are closed. This page separates their results to show the
evolution without combining different benches into an intelligence score.
Every figure retains its control, denominator and source report.

## ECHO-1 · memory, action and transfer

| Measure | Result |
|---|---:|
| Acceptance tests | 488 |
| Canonical trace | 352 turns |
| Known T prediction | 99.68% over 312 turns |
| PatternMemory | 80/80 versus T at 40/80 |
| Transfer across worlds | aggregate +128 over scratch |
| Integrity | 0 false facts · 0 destroyed memories |

The danger policy changes from `[0,0,0]` to `[-12,+5,0]` for approach, avoid
and wait. ECHO-1 also closes objects, opening, delivery, controlled cortical
conflict and narration with no causal writes.

ECHO-2 retains ECHO-1 and adds death and respawn, learning from food and
poison, survival while retaining memory, distribution shift, patterns without
object or position IDs, streaming, consolidation, inherited predisposition and
joint energy-temperature regulation.

## Causal capacity comparison

| Exam | ECHO-1 reference / control | ECHO-2 | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Held-out perceptual signatures | 256 LIF: 829/2,048 | 512 LIF: 2,048/2,048 | +1,219 |

The first two rows come from CAPACITY-1 and remove or shuffle the tested cause.
The third uses different workloads: it shows exercised sequence scale, not
comparable accuracy. The neural architecture is a perceptual monitor; Q and the
gate retain causal control of the decision.

## How to read the result

ECHO-1 demonstrates the base cycle: represent, remember, predict, decide, act
and learn. ECHO-2 demonstrates that the cycle can persist across lives,
internal signals and longer sequences. It does not yet demonstrate flight
physics, real sensors, PX4, a physical drone or AKD1500; those steps belong to
ECHO-3.
