# E4-RELATION-1 · RELATION-A

22 September 2026 · A valid; RELATION-1 remains open

RELATION asks whether encounter history between agents can improve later decisions. “Shared” means both took part in an encounter, not that they share memory. Each agent keeps its own observation. Felt trust, a soul or subjective consciousness is not assumed.

## RELATION-A

Each observer stores factual episodes in its existing CAM: context, observed actions, tick, its own cost and output. Links traverse the global sequence or the sequence for an identified counterpart. Receipts and links use existing host fields; the 16-byte WSP layout is unchanged and E[6] remains zero. No other agent's Q is copied, and no second CAM, bus or policy is introduced.

Histories can be asymmetric and incomplete. Without identity, an encounter remains unattributed; a missing receipt is not converted into an assumed action. A proposal or attempt is not counted as success. Summaries are derived queries, not new facts or affect scores.

## Results

Three development seeds (113, 127 and 139), 144 rounds each: 96 training and 48 greedy. **912 primary episodes** were reconstructed across 7,440 native turns including controls, reversed order and partial observation.

Memory is passive in this increment: actions, costs, predictions and outcomes matched the no-recording control. Reversing order reproduced traces and digests. This confirms recording and reconstruction under the tested contract; it does not show that remembering improves the policy or that felt trust or relationship exists.

**36 new tests** and **181 distinct tests** including selected regressions passed. Checks covered capacity and ticks, separate identities, partial perspectives, corrupted links, write failures and reconstruction into CAMs with different addresses. If recording fails after an action, costs are preserved and the session stops; no physical rollback is claimed.

## What remains

RELATION-B must distinguish useful biography from decorative logging: transfer history to decisions in a new task. It will compare complete history with no-history, OTHER-only, grouped-identity and order-removed controls; match training, resources and opportunities; and count whether training cost is repaid. RELATION-C will test rupture/reunion and contradictory or incomplete histories. RELATION-D will be a new preregistered, audited examination.

Until B/C/D close, **RELATION-1 is not green**. A does not measure subjective trust, establish self-repair or consciousness, or change INTERACTION. No LLM was activated. [ECHO-4 roadmap](/en/docs/echoai/echo4) · [Article](/en/articulos/echo4-relacion-historia-medida).
