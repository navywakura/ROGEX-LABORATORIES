**ECHO-4 preserves and reconstructs its state within its tested continuity contract. DREAM-A now records and replays actual attempts.** Continuity has a bounded closure; DREAM is still an open experiment, not demonstrated self-improvement.

## A history that survives a process restart

The programme's fictional inspiration describes two anonymous characters whose encounters create a shared history. Their “universe” is that relationship. This is a research metaphor, not a physical law or evidence of souls.

Before studying relationships, the agent must retain its past. CONT-A preserved nominal state, CONT-B added active diagnosis and recovery, CONT-C distinguished resumption, new bodies, descendants and forks, and CONT-D tested them together.

The examination produced **69/69 exact checkpoints, 300/300 matching subsequent events, 186/186 matching native turns and 30/30 rejected mutations**. Reconstruction in fresh processes supports `continuity1_green=true`. This covers completed synchronous WORLD-1 operations under trusted custody—not arbitrary power failures, distributed exclusivity or subjective identity. Terminals do not revive; executor restoration is not self-repair.

## DREAM-A: recorded experience, not invented futures

Inspired by [Dream-RSI](https://dream-rsi.com), our first increment uses the original agent's native/greedy modes. It logs provenance and costs and reveals only recorded outcomes. Qwen remains off and weights are unchanged.

The development bank has **3 trees, 31 nodes and 28 new native attempts**. Its 62 replay queries return 28 observed outcomes, 32 unknowns and two terminal responses. An unrecorded continuation remains unknown. Replay matches the original traces without rerunning the environment or calling an LLM; reading and computation still cost resources.

This delivery's regression work covered **748 distinct tests: 747 passed and one historical expected failure**, after authorised retries of GUI tests initially blocked by the sandbox. These are software tests, not 748 scientific experiments or a global ECHO-4 certificate.

## What would make the next result exciting?

Can the same fixed-weight model achieve better results by allocating its exploration budget better? Improvement on fresh tasks against budget-matched controls would support better search—not automatically a better neural model, subjective experience or unlimited improvement.

A discriminating task and evaluator come first. DREAM-B will examine bounded declarative Qwen proposals, DREAM-C offline selection, and DREAM-D fresh trials, multiple cycles, controls and rollback. Candidates cannot rewrite the examiner, gate or core. **`dream1_green=false`.** OTHER, INTERACTION, RELATION and ROLES will investigate whether another agent and a shared history materially improve decisions; they remain pending.

## A distributable agent, not a Discord bot

Legacy `echo-discord` is excluded from active ECHO-4 development, including its tools, environment and models. The laboratory's community channel is separate from the agent architecture.

**E4-RELEASE-1** is planned after INTEGRATE and before hardware: packaging, clean installation, tests, licence/privacy review and authorised Hugging Face publication. The runtime will coordinate a fast core and optional slow cortex; packaging does not merge them into LLM weights. A stable software release and later laboratory hardware research can coexist. METAVERSE-1 stays last.

MAINTAIN's costly repair and useful-work tests remain pending. No current phase certifies subjective consciousness. The motivation is measurable: what changes when an agent retains a past, distinguishes its own influence and learns when interaction helps?
